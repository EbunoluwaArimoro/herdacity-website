Save

Save your board and set it as your lock screen.

How to set it on iPhone
  1. Tap Save Image (or press and hold the board, then Save to Photos).
  2. Open Photos, open your board, tap the share icon, then Use as Wallpaper.
  3. Pinch to fit, turn off Perspective Zoom or depth effect, then tap Set as Lock Screen.
How to set it on Android
  1. Tap Save. The board goes to your Gallery or Downloads.
  2. Open it in Gallery, tap the three dots, then Set as wallpaper.
  3. Choose Lock screen.
